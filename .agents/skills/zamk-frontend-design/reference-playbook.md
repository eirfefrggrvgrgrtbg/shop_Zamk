# Reference Playbook

This playbook defines principles to be studied from strong products. **This is NOT a list of products to copy pixel-for-pixel.**

When conducting any future design research, agents must explicitly state:
1. **REFERENCE**: The product being studied.
2. **OBSERVED PRINCIPLE**: The specific UX/UI principle identified.
3. **WHY RELEVANT TO ZAMK**: How it applies to our current context.
4. **HOW IT WILL BE ADAPTED**: The plan to implement the principle within ZAMK's design language.
5. **WHAT WILL NOT BE COPIED**: The aspects of the reference that do not fit ZAMK.

## Categories for Study

### Linear
Study Linear for:
- Strong information hierarchy.
- High data density without feeling overwhelming.
- Extreme reduction of visual noise (removing unnecessary borders and backgrounds).
- Precision in micro-interactions.

### Stripe / Ramp
Study Stripe and Ramp for:
- Financial and data hierarchy.
- Operational clarity for complex workflows.
- Displaying metrics and tabular data.
- Progressive disclosure of detail in complex forms.

### Raycast / Vercel
Study Raycast and Vercel for:
- High-level interaction polish.
- Command surfaces and global search interfaces.
- Compact, unobtrusive navigation.
- Top-tier keyboard quality and focus management.

### Shopify
Study Shopify for:
- Commerce operations and catalog/order workflows.
- Merchant-facing UX that balances ease of use with deep configuration.
- Managing states for complex entities (e.g., product variants, inventory).

### Fashion/Editorial References
Study high-end fashion e-commerce for:
- Typography choices that evoke a premium brand.
- Visual rhythm and pacing of content.
- Managing imagery (crop, scale, alignment).
- Brand character expression.
- The use of "premium whitespace" (space used deliberately to frame content, not just emptiness).
